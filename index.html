THE PHYSICS

Weight changes. Mass doesn't.

Weight = mass × gravity. Acceleration = net force ÷ mass. Switch worlds mid-flight to see the difference. Green arrows show your applied force; peach arrows show gravity.

THE FINE PRINT

A simulation, not physical resistance.

This custom cursor lives only in the playground; it cannot move your Windows pointer or resist your hand. Mouse speed maps to an artificial force capped at 2 N. No air drag or orbital physics; a flat surface, floor friction and adjustable bounce are modeled. Giant planets use a reference gravity with a fictional solid floor.
